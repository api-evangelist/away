At Away, we’ve always believed that your luggage should be the last thing you worry about when you’re out in the world. For years, our limited lifetime warranty was the gold standard, giving travelers peace of mind if their product didn’t stand up to our uncompromising manufacturing standards. But as travel has become more unpredictable, faster and more frequent, we compared luggage warranties, and saw a widening gap between two types of coverage: warranties that cover how a bag was made, and coverage that’s designed for how a bag is impacted by travel.
