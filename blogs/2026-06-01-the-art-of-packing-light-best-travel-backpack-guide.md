There is a specific kind of freedom that comes with skipping the baggage carousel entirely. You step off the plane, bypass the crowds waiting for their oversized suitcases, and walk straight out the front doors of the airport into a new city. No heavy lifting, no gate-check anxiety, just you, your plans, and everything you need carried comfortably on your back.
